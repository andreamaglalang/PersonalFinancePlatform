## Verification scope

Checked TrueType sfnt signature, table bounds, required outline/metadata tables, absence of fvar, OS/2 weight and non-italic flag, internal family/style/version, and Unicode cmap mappings.
All five faces map U+20B1 peso, U+2212 minus, digits 0-9, plus, hyphen, comma, period, parentheses, colon, semicolon, exclamation/question marks, straight/curly quotes, en dash and em dash to nonzero glyph IDs.
GSUB tnum feature tags were inspected. This does not establish active script/language lookup reachability, shaped digit advance equality, glyph appearance, or correct device rendering.
No font parser dependency installed. Metadata inspection used bounded in-memory table reads in PowerShell, not font execution. Outline validity is not a complete conformance/security audit.
Hashes record acquired bytes and post-write equality; no independently published signed digest was checked. Commit-qualified HTTPS URLs pin retrieval; they do not provide independent artifact authenticity attestation.
Device glyph/fallback checks, tabular-digit measurements, font-scale accessibility, and Android compilation remain separately authorized follow-up work.
